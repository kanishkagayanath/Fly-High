# Fly High 🚗

A free-roam driving game built with Unity and C#. Explore a coastal city, complete coin missions, and unlock vehicles.

Play directly on Android, or use your Android phone as a controller for the Windows game.

## Features

- Phone tilt steering with acceleration, brake, and reverse buttons
- A city and beach to explore
- Moving traffic and walking characters
- Morning, evening, and night lighting
- Coin-collecting missions and a vehicle shop
- Minimap, speedometer, music, and sound effects
- Pause menu, volume controls, and mobile car reset

## Download

Open the [Releases page](https://github.com/kanishkagayanath/Fly-High/releases) and download the files under **Assets**.

- **Android.apk** — Android game and PC controller
- **PC.zip** — Windows 64-bit game

## Play on Android

1. Download and install the APK.
2. Open Fly High and tap **PLAY ON PHONE**.
3. Hold your phone in landscape and tap **CALIBRATE** while holding it level.
4. Tilt left or right to steer.
5. Hold **ACCELERATE**, **BRAKE**, or **REVERSE** to drive.

## Play on Windows with your phone

1. Extract the Windows ZIP and run **FlyHighCity.exe**.
2. Install and open the Android APK.
3. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network.
4. On your phone, tap **PLAY ON PC** and select your PC.
5. Calibrate the steering and start driving.

Allow the Windows game through Windows Firewall on your private Wi-Fi network when prompted.

## Missions and vehicles

Drive to an **M** marker and slow down to start a mission. Follow the coins and the route shown on the minimap. Spend earned coins in the vehicle shop.
